I work at the intersection of wireless hardware and measurement science — running OTA, SAR, and EMC tests that tell you whether a device actually behaves in the real world.

At Element Materials Technology I operate spectrum analyzers, signal generators, and SAR systems to capture TRP, TIS, and RF exposure against FCC and industry standards, then turn those traces into reports that stand up in a regulatory packet.

I also write the glue: Python to automate sequences, C and Embedded C on STM32 and Raspberry Pi when hardware and software have to agree. Detail is the job.
